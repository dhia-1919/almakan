Eight concepts.
Eight identities.
One program behind them.

Every AlMakan concept has its own world, from neon Tokyo streets to a classic Italian dining room in Shaab Al Bahri. Here are uniform ideas for each one, front and back of house, and how a single program can run all of them across Kuwait, the UAE and Saudi Arabia.

01 The ideas

A look for every concept.

Pick a concept, then switch between front of house and kitchen. Each look starts from the concept’s own character, so guests feel the brand before they open the menu.

These are starting points drawn from each concept’s character. Final colours, fabrics and artwork follow each brand’s own guidelines and are developed together with your team.

02 One program

Eight brands. One way of running them.

Each concept keeps its own look. Underneath, everything that makes uniforms expensive to manage is shared, so a new concept or a new branch plugs straight in.

Unique to each concept

03 Built for working kitchens

Designed for service, not only for photos.

Open kitchens, rooftop grills and bakery ovens put real demands on what chefs and servers wear. Every piece is tested on staff before it goes into production.

Heat

Fabrics that breathe

Lighter, breathable blends for open kitchens, the Matchbox grill and summer terraces.

Stains

Colour that forgives

Darker aprons and stain-resistant finishes where sauce, soy and charcoal are part of the job.

Brigade

Role you can read

Name and role on every jacket, with subtle differences for head chef, sous chef and commis.

Wear

Replaced on time

Each piece has a set replacement cycle, so tired uniforms never reach the dining room.
